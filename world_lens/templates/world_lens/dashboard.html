Project thesis

Power depends on what counts.

World Ledger is a comparative strategy map of economic power. Its central claim is that no ranking of power is neutral: changing the importance assigned to markets, industry, trade, finance, population, institutions, demographics or resources produces a different ordering of the world. That exposes the theory of power embedded in the result.

Power Now shows where economic power sits today. It measures observable weight across domestic markets, productive capacity, trade reach, financial buffers and population, revealing both the scale of an economy and the structures through which that scale can be mobilised.

Power Potential shows where the conditions for greater economic power already exist. It measures observed momentum, reinvestment, demographic runway, conversion capacity, productive depth, macroeconomic resilience and resource optionality, revealing economies with the capacity to strengthen their position and those whose present power rests on weaker foundations.

    Method, scope, limitations and sources

    {{ dataset_version }} admits economies with complete coverage across every scored input, then selects the top {{ cohort_count }} under the equal-weight Power Now baseline. The same cohort is used for both rankings and every visual, so rank comparisons never switch denominators.

    Latest observations must be from 2020 or later; decade measures use at least five observations from {{ score_start }}–{{ score_end }}. Scale measures are log-transformed before standardisation. Every pillar combines exactly three standardised observations before the pillar receives a weight, limiting double counting while keeping the underlying evidence visible. Inflation and growth volatility are direction-reversed so higher contributions always mean greater stability. Scores are relative z-score composites, not cardinal quantities of power.

    Strategic-resource exports cover HS chapters 25, 26, 27, 44 and 71: minerals, ores, fuels, wood, precious metals and stones. Resource optionality does not measure underground reserves, military control, beneficial ownership or foreign corporate control; those remain roadmap questions. No IMF or other expert projection enters Power Potential. Data release: {{ data_updated }}.
